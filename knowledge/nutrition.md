# Nutrition tips

Quick and helpful tips from Fitness Abbas for a healthy addition to your diet plan.
These are general tips, not medical advice.

## Boiled chicken
Boiled chicken is simple, healthy and a great source of lean protein — a good choice when building muscle or losing weight.
Boil the chicken in water with salt and pepper, then simmer until fully cooked. Garlic, onions or herbs add flavor.
Use it in salads, soups or stir-fries.

## Bread salads
Mediterranean bread salads combine toasted bread cubes with fresh vegetables, herbs and a tangy dressing —
for example Greek-style (tomatoes, cucumbers, feta, olives) or Italian panzanella (tomatoes, onions, basil, mozzarella).
They are filling, use up stale bread, and work as a side dish or a main course.

## Dry fruits
Raisins, dates, apricots, figs and prunes are rich in vitamins, minerals and fiber, give quick energy
and help satisfy sweet cravings. Eat them as a snack or add them to salads, granola bars and trail mix.

## Healthy fruits
Berries (antioxidants), citrus fruits (vitamin C), kiwi (vitamin K), melons (vitamin A) and apples (fiber)
are low in calories and help with weight management — a great alternative to sugary snacks.
Add them to smoothies or salads, and eat a variety of colors.

## Fresh juice
Orange (vitamin C), apple (antioxidants), carrot (vitamin A) and beet juice (folate and potassium)
give a concentrated dose of nutrients and help with hydration. Add ginger or mint for extra flavor.

## Protein
Protein supports healthy muscles, bones and skin and helps you feel full.
Good sources: chicken, turkey, fish, eggs, dairy, beans and nuts.
Plant-based options: tofu, lentils, chickpeas, nuts and seeds.
