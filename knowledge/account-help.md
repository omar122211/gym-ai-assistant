# Account help

## Signing up and signing in
Sign up on the Fitness Abbas website with your full name, date of birth, email and a password.
Sign in with your email and password to open your member dashboard.

## Member dashboard
Your dashboard shows your progress charts. Your profile page shows your username, email, birth date
and your subscription length. You can choose a profile picture from your dashboard.

## Changing your password
When signed in, open "Change password", enter your old password, then the new password twice.

## Forgot your password?
For security, passwords cannot be reset just by entering an email address.
Please contact the gym administrator through the contact form to reset your password.

## Privacy
Passwords are stored securely (hashed) and are never shown on any page — not even to staff.
