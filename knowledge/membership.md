# Membership plans and prices

Fitness Abbas has three subscription plans. Choose the plan that suits you.

| Plan | Length | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter Edition | 3 months | $15 |
| Complete Edition (most popular) | 6 months | $30 |
| Ultimate Edition | 1 year (12 months) | $50 |

## Which plan is the best value?
- Starter Edition costs $15 for 3 months, which is $5 per month.
- Complete Edition costs $30 for 6 months, which is $5 per month. It is the most popular plan.
- Ultimate Edition costs $50 for 12 months, which is about $4.17 per month — the lowest price per month.

## How to join
1. Sign up on the Fitness Abbas website with your full name, date of birth, email and a password.
2. After signing up you are taken to the pricing page, where you choose Starter, Complete or Ultimate.
3. You can also press "Skip" to choose a plan later from your account.

Your current subscription (3, 6 or 12 months) is shown on your profile page.
