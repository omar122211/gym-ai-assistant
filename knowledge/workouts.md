# Workout programs

Fitness Abbas offers a variety of workout options so training stays interesting:

- **Cardio** — endurance training on cardio machines such as treadmills and bikes.
- **Bodybuilding** — muscle-building training with machines and free weights.
- **Powerlifting** — heavy strength training focused on lifts such as the squat.
- **Calisthenics** — bodyweight training (coached by Ezz).
- **Power training** — explosive conditioning work such as battle ropes.
- **Gymnastics** — flexibility, core strength and body control.

## Group classes
Group fitness classes include yoga, Pilates and cycling, led by experienced instructors.

## Getting started
If you are new, start with any program at your own level — the gym is designed for all fitness levels,
and the staff and personal trainers can guide you.
